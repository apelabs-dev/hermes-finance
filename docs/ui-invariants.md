# UI invariants

Scope: application pages and their sheets, drawers, panels and login states on synthetic databases; Chrome gates run pages at 390, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440px in light and dark, plus 430 and 360 for the tap check; sheets and panels run at a subset of those widths. Phone means below 1024px. Preserve the audit's selectors, exclusions and tolerances. The gate checks below are enforced by `tests/test_gates.py::test_gate_2_layout`, which runs `scripts/audit_layout.py` and its planted-bug self-test.

1. **Help:** bare info triggers are borderless 24×24px circles; labelled help is unboxed 13px text. Labels/masks use a 10px control gap (the tested dense exception is 6px). Enforced by `tests/test_help_option_a_footer.py::test_bare_help_trigger_is_a_borderless_24px_circle`, `tests/test_help_option_a_footer.py::test_labelled_help_is_readable_13px_text_without_a_box`, and `tests/test_help_option_a_footer.py::test_label_and_mask_sit_a_consistent_10px_from_their_control`.
2. **Phone hit targets:** controls need a visible or invisible 44×44px hit area, clipped to reachable bounds; the centre must receive the tap. Audit `tap` / `tap-covered`; help specifically: `tests/test_help_option_a_footer.py::test_a_phone_keeps_a_44px_invisible_hit_box`.
3. **Readable text:** visible text is at least 13px; ordinary text contrast is at least 4.5:1 (the audit's large-text exception is 3:1). Tab labels stay whole on one line; page content clears the phone tab bar. Audit `text`.
4. **Phone inputs:** editable text uses 16px to avoid focus zoom. Enforced for the Transactions filter by `tests/test_browser_p1.py::test_on_a_phone_the_drawer_is_a_sheet_and_the_order_is_a_chip`.
5. **Reduced motion:** retain fades only and disable number count-up. Enforced by `tests/test_motion_p3a.py::test_reduced_motion_keeps_fades_only` and `tests/test_browser_motion_p3a.py::test_reduced_motion_has_no_count_up`.
6. **Clipping:** ring labels neither wrap nor enter their arcs; text and money fit their boxes. Audit `clipping` / `cliptext`; intentional single-line ellipses follow the audit's exclusions.
7. **Alignment:** adjacent card tops, row icons, badge glyphs and right-aligned list amounts share their intended edges/centres. Audit `alignment`.
8. **Spacing:** cards share padding, titles respect it, grid gaps match, packed grids have no holes and siblings do not overlap. Audit `spacing`.
9. **Charts:** canvases and legends fit their containers and card padding; a one-slice doughnut has no notch. Audit `charts`.
10. **Light theme:** controls avoid dark fills and large dark arrow gradients; other boxes avoid near-black fills. Audit `darkbg`.
11. **Segments and switches:** segmented buttons have equal gaps and, when stretched, equal widths; switches are at least 36×20px with their knobs inside the track. Audit `segments`.
12. **Empty states:** messages are centred appropriately, leave no excessive (>120px) blank space and do not cover plotted chart points. Audit `empty`.
13. **Overlays:** sheets, panels, drawers, chat, sync popovers and toasts fit the viewport and remain uncovered; toasts clear the phone tab bar. Audit `overlays` (including its explicit scrollable-drawer exception).
14. **Horizontal bounds:** pages do not exceed the viewport even when overflow is hidden; elements stay inside their cards except intended scrollers. Audit `overflow` / `escape`.
15. **One-line controls:** buttons and labels do not unexpectedly wrap. Audit `wrapped`.
16. **Row rhythm:** side-by-side cards align their corresponding row contents within the audit's 2px tolerance. Audit `rows`.
17. **Text endings:** no lone fragment is stranded on a final line. Audit `orphans`, with its short-text and scoped exclusions.
18. **Headers:** card headers stay within their one-line strip so cards below align. Audit `headers`; also `tests/test_text_density.py::test_card_headers_stay_one_line`.
19. **Row names:** a list row's name stays on one line, so the rows of a list keep the same height; `.li.wrap` rows may wrap their names on purpose, except those that keep them on one line (the "No budget yet" rows). Audit `rowname` (planted-bug self-test); also `tests/test_budget_rows.py::test_name_stays_on_one_line` and `tests/test_budget_rows.py::test_reason_is_never_cut` (the overrides behind it: docs/css-load-order.md).
20. **Whole figures:** a figure (a currency amount, a percentage, a number with thousands separators or a decimal one) never loses a character to an ellipsis, a line clamp or a box that clips it; labels may truncate, and a name that merely holds digits ("Store 4521") is a label. Audit `cutnum` (each character measured; planted-bug self-test with a cut-figure plant for each of the three ways, and controls: a label with digits that ends in an ellipsis, a whole figure before a cut label).
21. **Live controls:** every visible, enabled control does something you can see when clicked (something appears, goes, moves or changes, the route changes, or it sends its change); the option already chosen in a set of options is exempt, and so is anything in `scripts/audit_controls.py` ALLOW (normally empty, each entry with its reason). Behavioural gate `deadctl`: `tests/test_gates.py::test_gate_3_controls` runs `scripts/audit_controls.py` on the full and nocaps DBs at 1440 and 390 on every page, after its self-test (a button with no handler and a "See suggestions" button whose panel stays hidden must be caught, a button that shows a panel must not); also `tests/test_gate_wiring.py::test_controls_gate_catches_its_plants`.
